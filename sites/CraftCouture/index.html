Our Story

Tradition, Reimagined for Everyday Life.

Craft Couture, from the House of Gowri, is a contemporary Indian fashion destination in Kokapet, Hyderabad — a place where the language of Indian textiles meets the ease of modern dressing.

Each piece in our wardrobe is shaped by a quiet respect for craft: considered silhouettes, thoughtful fabric choices and a design approach that honours heritage without being bound by it. The result is clothing that moves between occasions, moods and seasons with effortless grace.

This is fashion with a point of view — rooted in craft, designed for today.
