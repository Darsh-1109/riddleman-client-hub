FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our landscaping services.

What landscaping services does Baytown Landscaping offer?

We offer landscape design, landscape installation, irrigation solutions and tree services for residential and commercial properties. Contact us to discuss your specific project requirements.

Do you handle residential landscaping projects?

Yes, we work with homeowners on a variety of residential landscaping projects including front yard improvements, backyard transformations, garden design and outdoor space enhancements.

Can commercial properties enquire about landscaping?

Absolutely. We provide landscaping services for commercial properties including office buildings, retail premises and other managed outdoor spaces. Reach out to discuss your property's needs.

Can I request irrigation-related services?

Yes, we offer irrigation planning, installation and maintenance services to help keep your landscape properly watered and healthy. Contact us to discuss your irrigation needs.

How do I arrange a landscaping consultation?

You can request a consultation by filling out our online contact form or by calling us at (281) 851-8684. We'll discuss your project and arrange a suitable time to connect.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Baytown, Crosby and surrounding communities including Highlands, Channelview, La Porte, Deer Park, Mont Belvieu and Greater Houston area. Contact us to confirm service availability for your location.

How can I request an estimate?

You can request an estimate by completing our online enquiry form or by calling (281) 851-8684. Provide your property details and project description, and we'll get back to you to discuss the scope of work.
