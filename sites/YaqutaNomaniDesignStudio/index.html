About the Studio

Design Is Not Just About How a Space Looks. It Is About How It Works and Feels.

"A designer by profession, a thinker by habit, and an inventor by choice."

Yaquta Nomani, a graduate of the Academy of Interiors INIFD, brings over five years of dedicated experience to every project. Having collaborated with varied organizations, the studio has cultivated a deep understanding of designing and executing residential and commercial spaces, alongside styling for magazine photoshoots and art installations.

We believe that good design is a key part of every structure. We strive to make each space safe, appealing, and profoundly functional, translating ideas into innovative design that honors the relationship between creativity, spatial thinking, material selection, and everyday living.

Our Approach

A Thoughtful Approach to Every Detail.

Understanding the Space

Study the proportions, layout, natural light and practical requirements of the space to establish a strong foundational strategy.

Understanding the People

Consider the client's lifestyle, routines, preferences and aspirations to ensure the design serves its inhabitants authentically.

Exploring Materials

Explore finishes, textures, surfaces and colours to establish a coherent, tactile design language that ages gracefully.

Balancing Form and Function

Bring together aesthetics, usability, storage, movement and comfort without compromising on visual harmony.

Refining the Details

Pay meticulous attention to furniture proportions, lighting, joinery and the smaller elements that complete a space.

Process

From an Idea to a Space You Call Your Own.

1. Initial Conversation

Understand the space, requirements and design aspirations through an open, collaborative dialogue.

2. Site & Space Understanding

Review the existing conditions, measurements, and practical considerations of your property.

3. Concept Development

Explore the design direction, moodboards, spatial layouts, and material possibilities.

4. Design Refinement

Develop detailed layouts, finalise selections, and refine visual details for approval.

5. Design Coordination

Coordinate the agreed design information, specifications, and relevant next steps with partners.

6. Project Completion

Support the final stages, styling, and handover according to the studio's agreed scope.

Principal Designer

Meet Yaquta Nomani.

Yaquta Nomani has graduated from the Academy of Interiors INIFD, Indore, and has been working in the field of interior design for more than five years. She has worked with three varied organizations to date, gaining comprehensive knowledge in designing and executing residential and commercial spaces, along with styling for magazine photoshoots and art installations.

As the founder of Yaquta Nomani Design Studio, she specializes in translating ideas into innovative design. The studio understands that good design is a key part of every structure, striving to make each design safe, appealing, and functional.

Her creative perspective is rooted in the belief that spaces should not only be visually compelling but should also deeply support the routines, aspirations, and well-being of the people who inhabit them.
