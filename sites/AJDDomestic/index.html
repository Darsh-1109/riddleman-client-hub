Our Process

From First Enquiry to Safe Installation.

A straightforward four-step process covering initial assessment, quotation, installation, scheduled inspections and dismantling.

01

Tell Us About Your Project

Share your site location, project type, elevations and access requirements by phone or through our quotation form.

02

Site Assessment

AJD assesses project requirements, ground conditions, access constraints and risk factors—including a site visit where needed.

03

Quotation and Planning

We prepare a clear, no-obligation quotation and determine the appropriate scaffolding configuration and programme.

04

Installation and Handover

Scaffolding is erected safely, inspected and handed over as agreed—with ongoing inspections and prompt dismantling on completion.
