The Studio

Thoughtful Design.
Timeless Spaces.

Studio Wodehouse is a multidisciplinary interior design and architecture practice based in Mumbai. Founded in 2014, the studio brings together international design sensibilities and Indian craftsmanship to create considered spaces that balance individuality, beauty, and functionality.

We believe that true luxury lies in the details: the proportion of a room, the tactile quality of a material, the way natural light moves through a space, and the seamless integration of bespoke furniture. Our approach is holistic, guiding each project from initial conceptualisation through meticulous technical detailing and final execution.

Philosophy

Designed With Intention.
Created With Purpose.

01

Understanding How People Live

Every project begins with a deep understanding of the people, their routines, needs, aspirations, and relationship with the space. Design is not imposed; it is discovered.

02

Thoughtful Spatial Planning

We focus on proportion, circulation, functionality, and the interplay of natural light. The relationship between spaces is carefully choreographed to feel both expansive and intimate.

03

Materials and Craftsmanship

We bring together honest materials, refined finishes, bespoke furniture, and traditional Indian craftsmanship to create a cohesive, tactile environment that ages gracefully.

04

Design Through Execution

We maintain strict continuity between the original design vision, technical detailing, site coordination, and final execution, ensuring the completed space mirrors the initial intent.

Leadership

Meet the Vision Behind the Studio.

Shonali Mahajan is the Founder and Principal Designer of Studio Wodehouse. Holding a Master's in Interior Design from the prestigious Domus Academy in Milan, she brings a wealth of international experience from established architectural and product design practices in both India and Europe.

Since founding the studio in 2014, Shonali has cultivated a practice renowned for its perfect blend of international design standards and authentic Indian craftsmanship. Her work has been featured in leading design publications, celebrated for its quiet luxury, spatial intelligence, and unwavering attention to detail.

"Design is not just about how a space looks, but how it supports and elevates the way we live. Every material, every proportion, and every detail must serve a purpose."

— Shonali Mahajan, Founder & Creative Director

Partnerships

Different Perspectives. Shared Design Thinking.

Studio Wodehouse frequently collaborates with artisan workshops, material innovators, and design practices to push the boundaries of residential design. Notable collaborations include the "Modern Maharaja" installation exploring heritage and folklore, and joint ventures with architectural practices to deliver holistic, art-centric transitional homes.
