The Bodega39 Experience

An Experience Beyond the Ordinary.

Bodega39 is a contemporary restaurant and cocktail destination where the art of dining meets the craft of the pour. Set within Oberoi Garden City in Goregaon East, we bring together modern European cuisine, thoughtfully composed cocktails and an atmosphere designed for conversation, celebration and lingering a little longer than planned.

From a relaxed afternoon plate to an evening of signature drinks and shared plates, every visit is shaped by the same attention to detail — in the kitchen, behind the bar and at the table.

Contemporary Dining Modern European and global cuisine, composed with care.
Cocktail Craft Signature pours and timeless classics, served with style.
Evening Atmosphere An inviting lounge setting for long conversations.
Social Spaces Thoughtful seating for couples, friends and groups.
