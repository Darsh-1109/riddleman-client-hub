Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about solar energy and our services.

On-grid systems are connected to the local electricity grid, allowing you to draw power when needed and export excess solar energy (net metering). Off-grid systems are completely independent, relying on battery storage to provide power without any grid connection, ideal for remote locations.
Most rooftops are suitable, but it depends on factors like structural strength, available shadow-free area, and roof orientation. Our team conducts a detailed site survey to assess feasibility and recommend the best configuration.
System capacity is determined by analyzing your average monthly electricity consumption (in kWh), available rooftop space, and future energy needs. We provide a detailed assessment during the consultation phase.
A solar inverter converts the direct current (DC) electricity generated by solar panels into alternating current (AC) electricity, which is the standard format used by home appliances and the electrical grid.
Yes, we specialize in industrial solar solutions, including large-rooftop installations and ground-mounted solar plants designed to handle high energy consumption and reduce operational costs.
A hybrid system combines the benefits of both on-grid and off-grid systems. It remains connected to the grid but also includes battery storage. This allows you to store excess solar energy for use during power outages or peak tariff hours.
To provide an accurate quotation, we typically need your recent electricity bills, the type of property (residential, commercial, industrial), available rooftop or land area, and your primary goals (e.g., cost savings, backup power).
Yes, as a full-service EPC provider, we handle everything from initial design and professional installation to testing, commissioning, and ongoing after-sales maintenance and support.
