Our Story

Some Moments Deserve to Be Savoured.

Kshana Coffee began with a simple idea — that a good cup of coffee deserves a good space around it. A place where the morning feels slower, conversations feel longer, and the ordinary pause between tasks becomes something worth holding on to.

Nestled near Durgam Cheruvu in Madhapur, we are a contemporary café built around the everyday rituals that make a day feel lived: the first sip, the shared plate, the quiet corner, the long afternoon.

We don't chase trends. We pour carefully, plate thoughtfully, and leave room for the moment to breathe. That, to us, is the Kshana experience.

— A space for the moments in between.

Coffee Culture & Craft

The Quiet Art of a Good Cup.

Coffee is more than a drink here. It's a ritual — one we take seriously, and invite you to slow down for.

The Pull

A good espresso is measured in seconds. We watch every shot — because the details are the drink.

The Pour

Milk, texture, timing. A quiet conversation between barista and cup — every latte a small piece of craft.

The Pairing

A bright espresso with a buttery croissant. A cold brew with a slice of chocolate cake. Coffee, and the moments it completes.
