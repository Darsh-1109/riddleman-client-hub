Six system types.
One engineering standard.

From grid-tied residential rooftops to utility-scale solar plants — we design, supply and install every major category of solar system with the same rigour and accountability.

SYS / 01

Grid-Tied Solar Systems

Connected directly to the electricity grid. Excess power is fed back, reducing bills through net metering — ideal for homes and businesses with stable grid access.

On-Grid Net Metering No Battery
SYS / 02

Off-Grid Solar Systems

Fully independent power systems with battery storage — designed for locations without reliable grid access or where complete energy independence is required.

Independent Battery Remote Sites

Hybrid Solar Systems

Combines grid connection with battery backup — delivering uninterrupted power during outages while optimizing solar generation and grid usage.
