A Passion for Cats. A Place Built Around Their Happiness.

Hi, I'm Anamta, the founder of Puffle Pets. My journey began with a simple belief: cats deserve more than just a cage when their parents are away. They deserve a home.

Understanding feline personalities takes patience, observation, and genuine affection. Every cat that walks through our doors is treated as an individual with unique habits, preferences, and quirks. Whether they are bold explorers or shy observers, we create an environment where they can thrive.

Building trust with pet parents is just as important to us as caring for the cats. We know how stressful it can be to leave your furry family member behind, which is why we prioritize transparency, regular updates, and open communication.
