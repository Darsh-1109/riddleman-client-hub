A Place to Connect

More Than a Café.
More Than a Game.

Some places are made for slowing down. Others are made for getting moving. At 24 Roots, you get both.

Start with a beautifully prepared coffee, stay for something delicious, and make room for a little friendly competition. We built this place around the simple idea that good company deserves good coffee — and a little room to play.

Whether you're here for a slow-bar pour-over, a long brunch with friends, or a competitive pickleball match under the open sky, 24 Roots is designed to feel like somewhere you want to return to.
