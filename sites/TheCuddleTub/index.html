Pet Care Tips

A Little Care Goes a Long Way.

Helpful, practical advice to keep your pet's coat, skin and paws in great shape between grooming visits.

How often should pets be groomed?

Frequency depends on breed, coat type and lifestyle. Most dogs benefit from a professional groom every 4–8 weeks, while regular brushing at home keeps the coat in between.

Why is coat brushing important?

Regular brushing removes loose hair, prevents tangles, distributes natural oils and helps you spot skin issues early. It's one of the simplest ways to support coat health.

How to maintain paw hygiene

Wipe paws after walks, check for cuts or debris, and keep nails trimmed to a comfortable length. Overgrown nails can affect posture and cause discomfort.

Caring for long-haired coats

Long coats need frequent brushing to prevent mats, especially behind the ears, under the legs and around the chest. A good comb and patience go a long way.

What to expect at a grooming appointment

A typical session includes a consultation, bath, blow-dry, brush-out and any agreed trimming or finishing. The groomer will explain each step and check in with you.

Preparing your pet for grooming

A short walk beforehand can help your pet relax. Let us know about any sensitivities, health concerns or preferences so we can tailor the session.
