About Brightwell Corporation

Engineering Capabilities. Manufacturing Excellence. Reliable Execution.

Brightwell Corporation Private Limited is a Pune-based industrial solutions company engaged in turnkey projects, manufacturing and engineering services.

With capabilities spanning electrical systems, structural fabrication, industrial piping, material handling and infrastructure solutions, the company works to deliver reliable products and integrated industrial requirements. Supported by procurement, production, quality testing, research and development, warehousing and sales operations, Brightwell focuses on delivering solutions that meet customer requirements.

With business roots dating back to 2005, Brightwell Corporation has steadily grown into a trusted name in industrial engineering and manufacturing in Maharashtra.
