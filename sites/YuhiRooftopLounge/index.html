The Yuhi Experience

A Different Perspective on Dining.

Perched on the eighth floor of Supreme Business Park in the heart of Hiranandani Gardens, Yuhi is a rooftop restaurant and lounge crafted for those who seek more than a meal — an evening that lingers.

From Asian and Japanese-inspired plates to Italian classics, North Indian favourites and a thoughtfully curated bar, every detail is composed to turn a dinner into a destination.
