About the Studio

Every Detail Tells a Story.

SD Decoration and Events is a creative event-decoration service based in Kamothe, Navi Mumbai, focused on bringing celebration ideas to life. We believe every occasion deserves thoughtful styling — from the first floral arrangement to the final finishing touch.

Our approach is simple: listen carefully, design thoughtfully, and execute with care. Whether it's a wedding stage, a birthday backdrop or an intimate anniversary setup, we work closely with you to shape a celebration that feels genuinely yours.
