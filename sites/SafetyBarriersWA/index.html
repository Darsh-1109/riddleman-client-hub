Committed to Safer Infrastructure.

Safety Barriers WA (Yarnell Pty Ltd T/as) is a dedicated provider of professional safety barrier and commercial construction solutions. Based in Toodyay, Western Australia, we serve the infrastructure sector with a focus on dependable, high-quality project support.

Our approach is rooted in safety-first thinking and practical engineering solutions. We understand that protecting road users, workers, and critical infrastructure requires meticulous planning, quality workmanship, and reliable communication. We partner with commercial construction companies, civil contractors, infrastructure developers, and local authorities to deliver solutions that meet the rigorous demands of Western Australian projects.
