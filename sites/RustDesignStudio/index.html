About The Studio

Design Is More Than
What You See.

At RUST, we explore the relationship between people, spaces and materials to create distinctive architectural and interior experiences that balance functionality, visual character and environmental sensitivity.

Established in Rajkot in 2005, our practice is rooted in the belief that meaningful design emerges from a deep understanding of context, craft, and the everyday rituals of those who inhabit the spaces we create.

Principal Designer & Creative Director

Our Approach

Thoughtful Design.
Respect for Materials.
Spaces with Meaning.

We believe that architecture and interiors should age gracefully. Our material palette favours natural stone, honest concrete, warm wood, and reclaimed elements that tell a story over time.

Every design decision is weighed against its environmental impact and functional longevity. We do not chase fleeting trends; we design for the decades ahead.
