The Studio

A Practice Rooted in Curiosity and Expression.

Founded in 2013, Quirk Studio is a boutique interior design practice based in Mumbai. We approach every project as a distinct creative exercise, balancing spatial functionality with artistic expression and personal identity.

Our philosophy centres around creating people-centric spaces that combine functionality, comfort, timeless design, and individual expression. We do not apply a fixed style; instead, we develop a distinctive design narrative for each space.

Our Approach

No Two Spaces Should Tell the Same Story.

Individuality

Every project reflects the unique purpose, personality, and narrative of the people behind it. We reject cookie-cutter design.

Functionality

A beautiful space must work naturally. We prioritize intuitive spatial planning that enhances daily life without compromising on aesthetics.

Materiality

Materials, finishes, colours, and textures are the vocabulary of our design. We explore tactile combinations to create depth and character.

Experimentation

We embrace the unexpected. By playfully combining form, colour, and spatial composition, we discover truly original solutions.

Timelessness

We design for longevity. Our spaces retain their identity and relevance, gracefully existing beyond temporary design trends.
