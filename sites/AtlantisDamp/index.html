About Atlantis

Understanding the Problem. Delivering the Right Solution.

Atlantis Damp Proofing & Timber Specialists is a specialist property preservation service based in Sandiway, Northwich. We work with homeowners and property owners across Cheshire to investigate, diagnose and address damp and timber-related issues.

Our approach is diagnosis-led. We take time to understand the symptoms, investigate the likely causes, explain the available options and recommend appropriate remedial work — rather than applying a standard treatment to every property.

Damp Proofing Services

Specialist Solutions for Damp-Related Issues

From rising moisture to condensation build-up, we investigate and address the full range of damp-related problems affecting properties across Cheshire.

Rising Damp

Rising moisture can affect walls, plaster and internal finishes. We investigate the likely cause and recommend appropriate treatments to help address the problem.

Arrange an assessment

Penetrating Damp

Moisture entering through external walls, roofs, joints or other building defects. We investigate the entry point and recommend suitable remedial approaches.

Arrange an assessment

Condensation Control

Excess moisture accumulation, inadequate ventilation and condensation-related issues can affect comfort and finishes. We assess the property and advise on management options.

Arrange an assessment

Damp Surveys

Professional property inspections and assessments to help identify the nature and potential causes of damp. A clear, written explanation of what we find.

Timber Preservation & Treatment

Protecting the Timber That Supports Your Property.

From fungal decay to wood-boring insects, we investigate and treat timber-related defects with appropriate, targeted remedial work.

Wet Rot Treatment

Investigation and treatment of timber affected by excessive moisture and fungal decay. We identify the moisture source and address the affected timber.

Arrange an assessment →

Dry Rot Treatment

Specialist assessment and remedial work for dry rot-related timber damage. Thorough investigation to locate the extent and address the underlying cause.

Arrange an assessment →

Woodworm Treatment

Inspection and treatment of wood-boring insect infestations affecting structural and joinery timber. We confirm active infestation before recommending treatment.

Arrange an assessment →

Timber Repairs

Assessment and repair or replacement of damaged timber where confirmed as part of our offering. We work to preserve as much original timber as is appropriate.

Arrange an assessment →
