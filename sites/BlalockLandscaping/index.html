When Water Becomes a Problem, the Right Solution Matters.

Houston's heavy rainfall exposes weaknesses in property drainage. We don't apply temporary cosmetic fixes; we solve the root cause at the property level.

Standing Water

Puddles and saturated soil that linger for days after heavy rainfall, damaging grass and creating mosquito habitats.

Foundation Threats

Water collecting near the home's foundation, leading to potential structural issues and slab leaks.

Runoff & Erosion

Uncontrolled water flow washing away topsoil, mulch, and newly planted landscapes.

Gutter Discharge Issues

Downspouts emptying directly next to the foundation or into already saturated yard areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

Standing water is typically caused by poor soil composition (like Houston's heavy clay), inadequate yard slope (grading), clogged or misdirected gutter downspouts, or a lack of proper drainage infrastructure to handle heavy rainfall.

Underground systems use a network of perforated or solid pipes buried beneath the surface. They collect excess water from problem areas (via catch basins or French drains) and safely channel it away from your home to a street curb, drainage ditch, or other approved discharge point.

A French drain is a specific type of drainage system consisting of a trench filled with gravel and a perforated pipe, designed to intercept and redirect groundwater. "Storm drainage" is a broader term that encompasses all systems designed to manage rainwater runoff.
