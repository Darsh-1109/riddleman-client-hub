01 — The Studio

Good Design Is More Than What You See.

At White Square Interiors, we believe a thoughtfully designed home should reflect the people who live in it.

From spatial planning and material selection to the finishing details, our approach focuses on creating spaces that balance beauty, functionality and everyday comfort.

Whether you are envisioning a complete home transformation or looking to elevate a particular space, we approach every design with creativity, care and a personal touch.

02 — Services

Designing Every Corner of Home.

Four ways we work with homeowners — from complete residential interiors to a single considered detail.

01

Residential Interior Design

Thoughtfully planned residential interiors that make the most of your space. From layout planning and material selection to furniture, lighting and finishes, we help bring your vision of home to life.

02

Interior Decoration & Styling

Give your home its character through carefully selected colours, furniture, textures, lighting and accessories. We focus on the details that make a space feel complete.

03

Home Design & Execution

From design concepts to bringing ideas into reality, we help coordinate the journey of transforming your space. Our approach focuses on design clarity, practical planning and attention to finishing details.

04

Personalized Design Consultation

Every home and homeowner is different. We offer personalized design consultations to understand your requirements, explore possibilities and help you make informed decisions about your space.
