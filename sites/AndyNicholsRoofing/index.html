Repairs & Storm Damage

When Your Roof Needs Attention, Start With the Right Assessment.

Understanding the extent of damage before choosing between repair and replacement can significantly affect the outcome, cost and longevity of the solution.

  • 01

    Roof Leaks

    Identifying the source of water intrusion, which is often distant from where the leak appears indoors.

  • 02

    Missing or Damaged Shingles

    Wind, hail and age can dislodge or break roofing materials, exposing the substrate below.

  • 03

    Storm-Related Damage

    Hail impacts, wind uplift and debris strikes can cause damage that is not immediately visible.

  • 04

    Roof Deterioration

    Age-related wear affecting membranes, flashing and structural components over time.

  • 05

    Repair vs. Replacement Assessment

    Evaluating whether localised repairs are sufficient or whether replacement serves the property better long-term.
