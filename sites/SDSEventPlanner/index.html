Planning an Event? Let's Answer a Few Questions.

What types of events does SDS Event Planner organise?
We organise a wide range of events including kids' birthday parties, theme parties, society functions, corporate events, and wedding-related celebrations.
Do you provide theme-based decoration and entertainment?
Yes, we specialise in creative themed decoration and can arrange various entertainment options such as anchors, magicians, character appearances, and interactive activity stations, subject to availability.
Do you handle catering and gifting arrangements?
Yes, we can coordinate catering setups and curate gifting options for your guests. We work with trusted partners to ensure quality presentation and delivery.
Which locations do you serve?
We primarily serve Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Lonavala, and Pune. Events in other locations can be discussed based on availability and requirements.
How early should I contact you to plan an event?
We recommend reaching out at least 3–4 weeks in advance for standard events, and 2–3 months in advance for large-scale weddings or corporate gatherings to ensure the best availability of venues and artists.
How can I request an event quotation?
You can fill out the enquiry form below, WhatsApp us directly, or call us. We will discuss your requirements and provide a customized proposal.
