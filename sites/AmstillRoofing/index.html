About Amstill Roofing

A family-owned legacy of protecting Houston homes since 1974.

Founded in 1974 by Frank Stilley, Amstill Roofing began with a simple mission: to install the best residential roofing systems in Houston with unwavering integrity. Over five decades later, that mission remains unchanged.

As a family-owned and operated business, we understand that a roof is more than just shingles and underlayment—it's the primary shield protecting your family, your memories, and your most significant investment. We combine old-school craftsmanship with modern roofing technology to deliver results that stand the test of time.

Our Mission

To provide unparalleled roofing quality and customer service, treating every home as if it were our own.

Our Values

Integrity, craftsmanship, transparency, and a deep respect for the communities we serve.

Our Promise

We stand behind our work with robust warranties and a commitment to your complete satisfaction.
