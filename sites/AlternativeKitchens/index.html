Established 1991

Spaces That Feel Like Yours.

Alternative Kitchens is a family-owned and operated business based in Malaga, Western Australia. For over three decades, we have specialised in the custom design, manufacturing, and installation of bespoke interiors.

We believe your home should reflect your individual style while supporting your everyday functionality. Our philosophy centres on thoughtful storage, material quality, careful planning, and meticulous attention to detail.

Every project is shaped entirely around your requirements. We don't force clients into standardised designs; instead, we collaborate to create custom solutions that elevate the way you live.

Our Journey

From First Ideas to Finished Spaces.

Having design, manufacturing, and installation under one roof ensures a seamless, coordinated project journey.

01

Understand

We begin by learning about your lifestyle, preferences, spatial requirements, and vision. Every great design starts with listening.

02

Design

Our designers develop a custom layout considering flow, cabinetry configuration, material selections, and functional details.

03

Create

Your project progresses to our manufacturing facility, where skilled craftspeople bring the design to life with precision and care.

04

Install
