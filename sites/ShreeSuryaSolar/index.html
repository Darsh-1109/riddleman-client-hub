Rooftop solar and PM Surya Ghar.

India's PM Surya Ghar scheme supports residential rooftop solar. Eligibility, subsidy amounts, approved vendors, capacity limits, DISCOM processes and documents depend on current government rules and can change.

We do not guarantee subsidy approval or any amount, and we do not claim authorised-vendor status for any scheme. Please check the official portal.
