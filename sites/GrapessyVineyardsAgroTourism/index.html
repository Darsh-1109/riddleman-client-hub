About Grapessy

More than a stay.
A place to experience.

Grapessy Vineyards Agro Tourism is a distinctive countryside getaway located in the heart of Junnar, Maharashtra. We combine vineyard-inspired hospitality with authentic agro-tourism experiences.

Whether you are seeking a quiet weekend retreat, a fun-filled family holiday, or a unique venue for your next gathering, Grapessy offers a relaxing break from everyday routines. Discover nature, enjoy recreational spaces, and create memorable moments with the people who matter.

Vineyard Experiences

Discover the experience behind the vines.

Immerse yourself in the art of winemaking. Our guided experiences offer a glimpse into the vineyard process, tasting sessions, and seasonal agricultural activities.

Vine-Making Process Tours

Walk through the vineyard and learn about the journey from grape to glass. Our guided tours explain the cultivation, harvesting, and basic winemaking processes in an engaging, educational format.

Wine Tasting Sessions

Savour a curated selection of wines in a relaxed setting. Our tasting sessions are designed to help you appreciate different flavour profiles, accompanied by insights from our knowledgeable staff. (Age restrictions apply).

Seasonal Fruit Stomping

A fun, hands-on agricultural experience for all ages. Participate in traditional fruit stomping during harvest season. Subject to seasonal availability
