Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our garden buildings and services.

We specialise in a comprehensive range of garden buildings, including garden sheds, garages, garden rooms, garden offices, log cabins, summerhouses, and gazebos. Each structure is crafted from high-quality timber to ensure durability and aesthetic appeal.

Absolutely. With over 50 years of experience, our team is happy to provide expert advice on the best building type, size, and materials for your specific garden space and intended use.

Yes, we offer professional delivery, fitting, and installation services. Our experienced team ensures your building is assembled correctly and securely, with minimal disruption to your garden.

Yes, as part of our site preparation service, we can safely dismantle and remove existing garden buildings, sheds, or structures to make way for your new installation.

Yes. We offer comprehensive site clearance and professional base laying services. A proper, level base is essential for the longevity and stability of any garden building, and we ensure this is done to the highest standard.

Yes, garden rooms make excellent home offices. They provide a dedicated, quiet workspace separate from the main house, helping to maintain a healthy work-life balance. We can discuss insulation and electrical options suitable for year-round use.

To provide an accurate quotation, we typically need your name, contact details, postcode, the type of building you are interested in, approximate dimensions, intended use, site access information, and whether you require installation.

While we offer a range of standard sizes, we can often accommodate specific dimensional requirements. Please contact us to discuss your custom needs, and we will advise on feasibility and pricing.

Planning permission requirements depend on the building's dimensions, location within your property, intended use, and applicable UK planning rules (Permitted Development Rights).
