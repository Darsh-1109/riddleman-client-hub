Decades of Growing. Generations of Experience.

Since 1977, Alfaro Tree Sales has been dedicated to growing and supplying quality trees. Founded by Erasmo Alfaro and his family, the business has developed through years of hands-on experience, a commitment to tree growing, and a passion for helping customers bring lasting greenery to their properties.

From our South Texas roots, we have grown into a comprehensive tree supply business, serving homeowners, landscaping professionals, contractors, and larger property projects across the region.

  • Family-owned heritage since 1977
  • Extensive tree farm and nursery operations
  • Specialized knowledge of Texas-adapted species
  • Dedicated support for residential and professional buyers
