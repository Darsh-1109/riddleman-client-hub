Who We Are

Landscapes Designed Around the Way You Live.

Bright Landscape Designs, Inc. is an owner-operated, full-service design-build landscape company. We specialise in transforming ordinary or neglected outdoor spaces into beautiful, functional, and personalised landscapes.

Every property has different requirements. A successful landscape balances aesthetics, functionality, careful plant selection, and the way you actually use your outdoor space. Since 1982, we have been serving the Greater Houston area with a commitment to creative thinking and thoughtful execution.

Comprehensive Services

From Concept to Living Outdoor Space

Landscape Installation & Planting

We bring landscape concepts to life through meticulous professional installation. Our team handles garden bed installation, landscape renovation, outdoor planting arrangements, and lawn improvements, ensuring every plant is selected and placed for optimal growth and visual harmony.

Explore Landscape Installation

Irrigation Solutions

Healthy landscapes need thoughtful watering. We provide comprehensive irrigation system planning, sprinkler system installation, and irrigation improvements. We carefully consider water distribution to ensure your landscape thrives efficiently, without overwatering or waste.

Enquire About Irrigation

Drainage Solutions

A beautiful landscape starts with a functional foundation. Proper drainage is critical in the Houston region. We address water collecting in garden areas, poor yard drainage, runoff affecting landscape beds, and saturated planting areas by incorporating smart drainage considerations directly into your landscape plan.

Discuss Drainage Requirements

Hardscaping & Stonework

Natural materials, beautifully integrated. We enhance outdoor spaces with carefully crafted stone pathways, garden edging, hardscape elements, and decorative stone features. Our stonework is designed to complement the planting and create seamless, functional outdoor surfaces.

Explore Hardscaping

Outdoor Landscape Lighting

Let your landscape shine after sunset. Strategic garden lighting, pathway illumination, and landscape accent lighting enhance both the safety and the evening beauty of your outdoor space, highlighting your planting areas and architectural features.
