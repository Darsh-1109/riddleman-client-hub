About Event Weavers India

A studio devoted to the art of celebration.

Based in Udaipur, we plan and design weddings that feel personal, considered and quietly extraordinary.

Our philosophy.

We believe a wedding is more than an event — it is a gathering of the people who matter most, held in a place that feels meaningful, shaped by details that reflect a couple's story.

Our approach is rooted in listening first. We take the time to understand your vision, your families and the experience you wish to create. From there, we shape a concept that is entirely yours — and we hold every detail in place so the celebration unfolds with ease.

We work with a carefully chosen network of artisans, designers and vendors across India — and we bring the same care to a 200-guest destination wedding as we do to an intimate ceremony.

What Guides Us

Three principles at the heart of everything we do.

— I

Considered Design

Every palette, every floral, every detail is chosen with intention — never by template.

— II

Personal Attention

We work with a small number of weddings each year so every couple receives our full focus.

— III

Seamless Execution

Our on-site team manages every moment so families can simply be present for the celebration.

Our Services

A complete studio for every chapter of your celebration.

From the first concept to the final farewell — we bring together planning, design and coordination so your wedding unfolds with grace.

Service 01

Destination Wedding Planning

End-to-end planning for destination celebrations — from venue selection and concept design to vendor curation and on-ground coordination.

We manage the entire journey: travel logistics, accommodation, welcome experiences, multi-day itineraries and the celebrations themselves. Every detail is held in place so you and your family can be fully present.

Service 02

Wedding Décor & Design

Bespoke décor direction shaped around your vision — mandaps, stage designs, floral installations, table styling and ambient lighting.

Our design team works with trusted artisans and florists to realise concepts that feel considered, personal and true to the venue and the season.

Service 03

Entertainment & Choreography

Curated performances, sangeet choreography, live artists and entertainment design that bring each evening to life.

From family performances to headline artists, we shape the programme so every evening has its own rhythm and its own memorable moments.

Service 04

Vendor Management

A trusted network of photographers, caterers, stylists and artisans — carefully selected and seamlessly coordinated.

We brief, schedule and manage every partner, so the people behind your celebration work together as one team.

Service 05

Guest Management & Hospitality

Travel, accommodation, welcome kits and on-ground hospitality — so every guest feels considered from arrival to departure.

Our hospitality team looks after the families and guests throughout, anticipating needs before they arise.

Service 06

Food & Beverage Coordination

Menu curation, chef briefings and dining experiences designed to complement the mood of each celebration.

From intimate family meals to grand banquets, we coordinate tastings, service flow and presentation with care.

Service 07

Transport & Logistics

Seamless movement of guests, families and teams across venues, airports and celebrations — handled with precision.

Fleet planning, arrival schedules and venue transfers are managed end to end, so nobody is left wondering where to be.

Service 08

Event Flow & Coordination

On-site direction of every moment — from baraat entry to final goodbye — so the celebration unfolds exactly as imagined.

Our team runs detailed run-of-show schedules and is present throughout, managing every transition behind the scenes.
