Architecture

Planning that works. Elevations that belong to their site.

Our architectural work begins with the practical realities of a plot, a programme and a budget. Efficient planning, clear circulation, well-considered core layouts and an honest reading of feasibility shape every scheme before the elevation is drawn.

From single residences to multi-storey residential projects, we coordinate the architectural design with structural and MEP inputs so that the building you see on paper is the building that can be built — and lived in — without compromise.

The Process

From first conversation to finished space.

Every project moves through a clear sequence — from understanding the brief, to planning, visualisation, technical development, execution and handover. The steps are flexible, but the discipline is consistent.

01

Discovery & Consultation

We begin by understanding your needs, aspirations, site and budget. This is a conversation, not a pitch — the brief shapes everything that follows.

02

Planning & Feasibility

The space, requirements and practical constraints are evaluated. Layout options, circulation and core positions are tested before any design is fixed.

03

Concept & Visualisation

Design directions, moodboards, layouts and 3D views are developed so the intended character of the space can be seen and discussed before work begins.

04

Technical Development

Drawings, detailing and relevant technical requirements are coordinated. This is the stage that turns a design into something that can be built accurately.

05

Execution & Coordination

Work, trades and site progress are coordinated according to the agreed scope. Design intent is protected through regular site attention and review.

06

Quality Review & Handover

Completed work is reviewed against the design and technical documentation. Final coordination and handover follow once the space meets the agreed standard.

Why Entitty

One team. One coordinated vision.

What distinguishes a considered practice is not a signature style — it is the discipline of keeping architecture, interior, technical and execution decisions in conversation with each other, throughout the project.

i

Integrated Architecture & Interior

Architecture and interior design are developed by the same team, so the plan, the elevation and the interior detail are decided together rather than handed between separate consultants.

ii

Principal-Consultant Approach

Clients work with one coordinated team across architecture, interiors, design and construction management — with a single point of responsibility for the design intent.

iii

Design & Execution Coordination

Design decisions are carried through to site. Coordination of trades, progress and quality checks keeps the work aligned with the drawings and the brief.

iv

Practical Space Planning

Layouts are shaped around how a household actually lives — storage, circulation, light and furniture placement are resolved before finishes are chosen.

v

Material & Visualisation-Led Development

Moodboards, material samples and 3D visualisation are used to develop and agree the design direction before work is committed to site.

vi

One Point of Coordination

For clients, the benefit is simple: one team, one conversation, one coordinated vision — from the first sketch to the final handover.
