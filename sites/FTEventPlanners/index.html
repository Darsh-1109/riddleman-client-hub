About the Studio

Your Moments.
Our Creative Touch.

FT Event Planners and Decorators — also known as Floral Truce — is a creative event planning and décor studio based in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai. We partner with couples, families and hosts to turn celebration ideas into beautifully executed experiences.

From the first conversation to the final detail, our approach is thoughtful, collaborative and deeply personal. We listen carefully, design with intention, and coordinate every element so you can simply be present for the moments that matter.

— FT Studio Planners · Stylists · Decorators
