The Destination

Discover the Beauty of Pawna.

Kalani Farms sits in the heart of the Pawna region — a landscape of still water, layered hills and quiet roads that feel a world away from the city.

Pawna Lake

A serene dam-side lake surrounded by the Sahyadris. Known for its calm waters, open skies and some of the finest sunsets near Lonavala.

Short drive from the property

Lohagad Fort

A historic hillfort with panoramic views of the Pawna valley. A gentle trek suitable for most visitors, and especially beautiful in the monsoon.

Nearby historic fort

Visapur Fort

Lohagad's larger neighbour, known for its massive walls, caves and sweeping views. A rewarding trek with plenty of history along the way.

Trek-friendly destination

Tikona Fort

A pyramidal peak overlooking Pawna Lake. One of the most accessible forts in the region, with views that stretch across the entire valley.
