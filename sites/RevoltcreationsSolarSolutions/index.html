Complete Solar Project Execution.
From Concept to Commissioning.

Our structured EPC methodology ensures rigorous planning, quality installation, and seamless handover.

01. Requirement Assessment

Understanding your energy consumption patterns, site constraints, and long-term sustainability goals to define the project scope.

02. Site Evaluation

Comprehensive structural, electrical, and environmental assessment to ensure optimal system placement and safety compliance.

03. Engineering & System Design

Detailed engineering drawings, shadow analysis, and electrical schematics tailored to maximize energy yield and structural integrity.

04. Procurement & Equipment Selection

Sourcing tier-1 solar modules, inverters, and mounting structures that meet stringent quality and performance standards.

05. Installation & Execution

Managed by experienced site engineers, ensuring safe, efficient, and code-compliant physical installation of all system components.

06. Testing & Commissioning

Rigorous performance testing, grid synchronization, and final handover with comprehensive documentation and training.
