R-03 Specialist Service

Protect Your Roof From Unwanted Intrusions.

Rat-proofing is offered as a listed Austin Roofing Contractors service. Rats commonly gain entry to homes through gaps along rooflines, eaves, soffits, vents and other building entry points. As part of roofing work — or as a standalone enquiry — we address these detailing points to help protect the roof and the spaces beneath it.

Rat-proofing is provided as roofing and building-entry detailing. It is not a licensed pest-control, chemical treatment or extermination service, and no elimination outcome is guaranteed.
