Storm Damage & Insurance

When Storms Hit,
Know What to Do Next.

Severe weather in Central Texas can be unpredictable. If you suspect hail or wind damage, acting methodically protects your home and your insurance claim.

  1. 1 Arrange a professional roof assessment with our team.
  2. 2 If damage is suspected, contact your insurance company to report it.
  3. 3 Allow the insurance adjuster to assess the damage on-site.
  4. 4 Compare the approved scope with our contractor's proposed work.
  5. 5 Discuss the repair or replacement plan with us before proceeding.

*Note: Insurance coverage and claim approval are determined solely by your insurer and policy terms.
