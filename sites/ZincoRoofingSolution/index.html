02 / About Zinco

More than roofing.
Complete building-envelope solutions.

Dombivli · Maharashtra

Zinco Roofing Solution is a roofing products and solutions provider working across roofing materials, insulated systems, structural frameworks and associated installation services. We help customers explore materials, select appropriate systems and execute roofing-related projects — from industrial facilities to residential properties.

Our work spans the full building envelope: metal roofing, insulated panels, structural steel components, ventilation systems and waterproofing. Every project is approached as a material and engineering problem — matched to the right system, the right profile and the right installation method.

/ 09How We Work

From planning to project completion.

A structured, consultative approach — from initial enquiry through design, material selection and on-site execution.

/ 01 Phase · Discovery

Planning & Preconstruction

Understanding project requirements, building type, span, load and environmental conditions — establishing the scope and constraints before design begins.

/ 02 Phase · Consultation

Consultation

Detailed discussion of roofing needs, material preferences, budget parameters and project timeline — aligning expectations and clarifying requirements.

/ 03 Phase · Design

Design & Material Selection

Selection of appropriate roofing systems, materials, profiles and accessories — matched to the building's structural and functional requirements.

/ 04 Phase · Assessment

Site Visit & Assessment

On-site inspection to verify measurements, structural conditions, access and installation requirements — ensuring accurate planning before execution.

/ 05 Phase · Execution

Installation & Execution

Professional installation of roofing systems, structural components and associated works — supervised execution with attention to detail and finish quality.
