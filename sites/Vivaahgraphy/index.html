We Don't Just Photograph Weddings. We Preserve How They Felt.

At Vivaahgraphy – The Wedding Studio, we believe that marriages are made in heaven, and our privilege is to capture them on Earth. Based in the heart of Juhu, Mumbai, our approach is deeply rooted in photojournalistic storytelling.

We look beyond the posed frames to find the unscripted moments: the quiet tear during the vows, the unrestrained laughter at the Sangeet, and the tender connections between families. With over 7 years of experience and a commitment to natural, timeless editing, we ensure your memories remain as vivid and authentic as the day they were created.

From our first conversation to the delivery of your final album, we ensure a seamless, personalized journey.

1. The Conversation

We begin by understanding you as a couple, your unique story, and your vision for the wedding day.

2. Creative Planning

3. The Celebration

Our passionate team arrives equipped and ready to capture genuine moments and important ceremonies with unobtrusive professionalism.

4. The Story Comes Together

5. Memories to Keep

We deliver your agreed digital photographs, cinematic films, and handcrafted albums, ready to be cherished forever.
