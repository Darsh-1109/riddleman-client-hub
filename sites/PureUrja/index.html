FAQ

Everything You Need to Know About Solar.

On-grid systems connect to the utility grid and generally do not have battery backup. Off-grid systems are entirely independent and rely on batteries. Hybrid systems connect to the grid but also include battery storage for backup during power cuts.

Most urban homes with reliable grid connectivity benefit from an on-grid system to reduce electricity bills. If you experience frequent power cuts, a hybrid system with battery backup is recommended.

Yes. Housing societies can install solar systems to power common areas like lifts, water pumps, and corridor lighting. The system size is determined by the society's shared consumption profile and available rooftop space.

We typically require your latest electricity bill (to understand consumption), your property address, photos of your rooftop, and your primary goal (e.g., bill reduction or backup power).

For safety reasons, standard on-grid inverters automatically shut down during a grid outage to prevent sending electricity back into the grid (anti-islanding). You will not have power unless you have a hybrid system with batteries.
